I. TUGAS HUKUM NEWTON

  • Soal 1: Rumus: Sigma F = m * a => a = F / m[span_30](start_span)[span_30](end_span). Diketahui m = 10 kg, F = 10 N[span_31](start_span)[span_31](end_span). a = 10 / 10 = 1 m/s^2[span_32](start_span)[span_32](end_span).
  • Soal 2: Rumus: s = v_0 * t + 0.5 * a * t^2, v_t = v_0 + a * t, F = m * a[span_33](start_span)[span_33](end_span). Diketahui v_0 = 0, t = 5 s, s = 5 m, m = 10 kg[span_34](start_span)[span_34](end_span). Hasil: a = 0.4 m/s^2[span_35](start_span)[span_35](end_span), v_t = 2 m/s[span_36](start_span)[span_36](end_span), F = 4 N[span_37](start_span)[span_37](end_span).
  • Soal 3: Rumus: a = (m_B * g) / (m_A + m_B), T = m_A * a[span_38](start_span)[span_38](end_span). Diketahui m_A = 3 kg, m_B = 5 kg, g = 10 m/s^2[span_39](start_span)[span_39](end_span). Hasil: a = 6.25 m/s^2[span_40](start_span)[span_40](end_span), T = 18.75 N[span_41](start_span)[span_41](end_span).
  • Soal 4: Rumus: a = (F * cos theta) / m, s = v_0 * t + 0.5 * a * t^2[span_42](start_span)[span_42](end_span). Diketahui F = 40 N, theta = 60 derajat, t = 6 s[span_43](start_span)[span_43](end_span). Hasil: a = 2 m/s^2[span_44](start_span)[span_44](end_span), s = 36 m[span_45](start_span)[span_45](end_span).
  • Soal 5 (Lift): Rumus: N = m * (g + a)[span_46](start_span)[span_46](end_span). Diketahui m = 50 kg, a = 4 m/s^2[span_47](start_span)[span_47](end_span). Hasil: N = 700 N[span_48](start_span)[span_48](end_span).
  • Soal 6 (Bidang Miring): Rumus: f_k = mu_k * N = mu_k * m * g * cos theta[span_49](start_span)[span_49](end_span). Hasil: f_k = 5 N[span_50](start_span)[span_50](end_span).
  • Soal 7 (Gesekan): N = m * g = 100 N[span_51](start_span)[span_51](end_span), f_s_max = 20 N[span_52](start_span)[span_52](end_span). F = 10 N (diam)[span_53](start_span)[span_53](end_span), F = 20 N (tepat gerak)[span_54](start_span)[span_54](end_span), F = 50 N (bergerak, a = 4 m/s^2[span_55](start_span)[span_55](end_span), s = 50 m[span_56](start_span)[span_56](end_span)).

II. TUGAS MATERI GERAK

  • Soal 1 (Berpapasan): Rumus: t = s_total / (v_A + v_B), s_A = v_A * t[span_57](start_span)[span_57](end_span). Hasil: t = 75 s[span_58](start_span)[span_58](end_span), s_A = 112.5 m[span_59](start_span)[span_59](end_span).
  • Soal 2 (Menyusul): Rumus: t_2 = s_1 / (v_2 - v_1)[span_60](start_span)[span_60](end_span). Hasil: t_2 = 1 jam[span_61](start_span)[span_61](end_span).
  • Soal 3 (Jatuh Bebas): Rumus: t = akar((2 * h) / g), v_t = g * t[span_62](start_span)[span_62](end_span). Hasil: t = 2 s[span_63](start_span)[span_63](end_span), v_t = 20 m/s[span_64](start_span)[span_64](end_span).
  • Soal 4 (GVA): Rumus: t_max = v_0 / g, h_max = (v_0^2) / (2 * g)[span_65](start_span)[span_65](end_span). Hasil: t = 1.2 s[span_66](start_span)[span_66](end_span), h_max = 7.2 m[span_67](start_span)[span_67](end_span).
  • Soal 5 (Gedung): Rumus: h = v_0 * t + 0.5 * g * t^2 => h = 30 m[span_68](start_span)[span_68](end_span).
  • Soal 6 (Kereta): Rumus: s = v_0 * t + 0.5 * a * t^2 => s = 84 m[span_69](start_span)[span_69](end_span).
  • Soal 7 (Rem): Rumus: t = v_0 / a => t = 6 s[span_70](start_span)[span_70](end_span).

IV. LATIHAN GELOMBANG & BUNYI

  • Konsep: Simpul (titik setimbang), Amplitudo (simpangan maks), 1 lambda (1 bukit + 1 lembah)[span_81](start_span)[span_81](end_span).
  • No. 2-3 (Tali & Danau): Rumus v = f * lambda. Hasil v = 2.5 m/s (tali)[span_82](start_span)[span_82](end_span), v = 0.8 m/s (danau)[span_83](start_span)[span_83](end_span).
  • No. 4 (Bunyi): f = v / lambda = 34 Hz[span_84](start_span)[span_84](end_span), T = 1 / f = 0.029 s[span_85](start_span)[span_85](end_span).
  • No. 5-6 (Tebing & Sonar): Rumus s = (v * t) / 2. Hasil s = 850 m (tebing)[span_86](start_span)[span_86](end_span), h = 1400 m (laut)[span_87](start_span)[span_87](end_span).
  • Frekuensi Bunyi: Infrasonik (< 20 Hz), Audiosonik (20 - 20.000 Hz), Ultrasonik (> 20.000 Hz)[span_88](start_span)[span_88](end_span).
  • Bunyi Pantul: Memperkuat bunyi asli, Gaung (kurang jelas), Gema (terdengar utuh)[span_89](start_span)[span_89](end_span).
